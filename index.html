FAQ

Common questions

How long does setup actually take?
For most Shopify stores, we go live within 48 hours of your onboarding call. We handle the entire build — WhatsApp Business API approval, Shopify integration, AI training, and testing. No technical knowledge required.
Do I need a WhatsApp Business API account?
No — we set that up as part of onboarding. The official WhatsApp API is required for automation at scale, and we handle the full verification process including any existing account migration.
Does this work for UK stores and SA stores the same way?
Yes — we serve both markets actively. UK clients billed in GBP, SA clients in ZAR. WhatsApp is the dominant channel in both countries and Instagram DM automation works identically. Support staff in both timezones.
What if a customer asks something the AI can't handle?
The AI escalates when outside its confidence threshold. It flags the conversation and notifies you immediately so a human can take over. You decide the escalation rules — we configure them during setup.
Are there contracts or lock-ins?
Monthly rolling — cancel anytime. We're confident in results so we don't need to lock you in. Most clients stay because the ROI is clear from month one.
How is this different from Shopify Inbox or Gorgias?
Shopify Inbox is reactive. Gorgias organises tickets but doesn't proactively sell. Leapnodes is proactive — it sends cart recovery messages, runs upsell conversations, and drives outbound campaigns. We're a revenue engine, not a helpdesk.
